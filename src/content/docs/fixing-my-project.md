## Hackatime Mistakes

This means that your project or the code in your Github do not match your Hackatime project. If its not this, or you think the reviewer made a mistake, ask in #back-to-basics-support.

## Journal Mistakes

This means that your journal was not up to our standards for some reason. Usually this would result in the project being rejected or graded lower, so see the reviewer note for more information.

This might also mean that your journal was generated with AI. If so, please go back and delete the entire journal and rewrite it yourself. Its ok if your English isn’t great, we just want to see that its your own work.
